## Suggested review workflow

1. Download and open the Excel workbook.
2. Inspect the source data, column definitions, date range, and missing values.
3. Review any formulas, PivotTables, charts, or dashboard elements in the workbook.
4. Recalculate key totals directly from the source data.
5. Add verified findings and business recommendations below after checking the workbook outputs.

## Business questions to document

- How do sales vary by day, month, and time period?
- Which product categories or items contribute most to sales?
- Are there recurring peak sales periods?
- What operational or product recommendations are supported by the data?
